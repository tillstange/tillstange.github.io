Narratives and Stem Cell Donor Availability: A Megastudy

with Michael Haylock, Patrick Kampkötter, Mario Macis, Yero Ndiaye, Axel Ockenfels, and Daniel Wiesen

Presented at AFE 2026 and EuHEA 2026.

We study how narratives shape consequential prosocial behavior in a large-scale field experiment with registered stem-cell donors (N = 59,368). In collaboration with DKMS, we test whether brief messages affect donor availability when a real patient match occurs. We find that well-intentioned appeals can backfire, underscoring the value of testing communication at scale before deployment.

Belief Formation in the Age of Generative AI

with Thomas Graeber, Ingar Haaland, and Chris Roth

We review how generative AI is reshaping the economics of belief formation. Drawing on emerging evidence, we examine the tension between improved access to information and new sources of distortion, including AI-generated content, human–AI interaction, personalization, and persuasion.
